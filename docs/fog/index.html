    Check with your own eyes

    Webcams (Dobogókő etc.) – idokep.hu. If Dobogókő looks down on a white sea, fog is present. Satellite: EUMETSAT View – check whether the Szentendre hills poke out of the white low-stratus layer Radiosonde Budapest (12843) – measured inversion, launched 00 and 12 UTC HungaroMet – official forecasts and fog warnings

    The fog base comes from airport observations (Ferenc Liszt LHBP, Budaörs LHBS, Tököl LHTL, all 20–35 km south), via the Iowa State Mesonet or aviationweather.gov. The fog top comes from the Open-Meteo forecast model (pressure levels and 80/120/180 m temperatures). Models often miss the exact height of the fog top by 50–150 m, so treat a "marginal" verdict as a coin flip.
